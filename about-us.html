The Brand

About Gregory Duane

"Craftsmanship meets legacy, and every thread weaves a story of sophistication and style."

Duane Glover, Founder

Gregory Duane is where craftsmanship meets legacy. Our journey begins with the heart and soul of our brand — Duane Glover, a man whose roots in sartorial tradition define the very essence of our luxury label.

Raised with an understanding that how you dress communicates who you are before you speak, Duane built Gregory Duane on a single belief: that the most important garment you own is the one made precisely for you.

Today, Gregory Duane serves clients across menswear, footwear, and bridal — all under one roof, all with the same exacting standard. Every commission is personal. Every stitch is intentional.

What We Stand For

Craftsmanship

We do not rush. Every garment is constructed by hand, inspected by eye, and approved only when it meets our exacting standard — however long that takes.

Bespoke

Nothing leaves our atelier that has not been made specifically for the person who commissioned it. No templates. No shortcuts. No compromises.

Legacy

We make garments that outlast trends — pieces that improve with wear, that carry stories, and that are built to be passed down to the next generation.
