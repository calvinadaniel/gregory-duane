Bespoke Tuxedos

The Bridal Atelier

Dressed for the
weight of memory.

A wedding tuxedo carries more than cloth. We approach each commission with the gravity it deserves — from the peak lapel to the final silk buttonhole, every decision is made with precision, intention, and the knowledge that this garment will be photographed more than any other you will ever own.

The Tuxedo Process

We begin months before the date. We finish when it's perfect.

01 — The Consultation

Vision First.

We discuss the ceremony, the venue, the season, and your vision. We photograph the setting when possible. Every decision — from lapel to lining — flows from this foundation.

02 — The Design

Every Detail Decided.

Peak or shawl lapel? Grosgrain or satin facing? One button or two? We guide you through every decision with editorial precision and no pressure toward the obvious choice.

03 — The Fitting

Move Freely. Stand Tall.

Multiple fittings ensure the tuxedo moves with you on one of the most photographed days of your life. We do not stop until it is exactly right.

Reserve Your Wedding Commission

Tuxedo commissions require a minimum of 12 weeks. Begin early — begin right.
